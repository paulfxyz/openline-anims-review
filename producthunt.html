Questions we were going to be asked anyway

The fine print, up front

All of it, rather than two lines under a button.

When can I use the code?

Straight away. The guaranteed 10% is live the moment it is issued and does not wait for launch day. Only the judged uplift is decided later.

What is the 100% actually worth?

A free year of Openline+, up to $1,000. If your plan costs less, the discount covers the plan; it is not paid out as the difference.

Can I claim more than once?

One reward per person. If you do several things, send the best link — we will look at the account behind it and judge the whole contribution.

What if my post is critical?

It is still eligible. Honest criticism is judged on the same ladder as praise. Bought or incentivised posts are the only thing we refuse.

How long does the review take?

A person reads every claim, usually within two hours during launch week. You get a reply either way, with the reason for the tier.

Can the discount be taken away?

No. Review can only raise it. The code you were issued keeps working even if we decide the post does not earn an uplift.
