Why a market

A rate card is a guess.
A market is a price.

Sign a rate card in October and you are still paying October's price in September. Wholesale capacity gets cheaper through the year. Your contract doesn't.

  • Quoted continuously

    Every route carries a live bid and ask. The price moves when conditions move, not when the contract comes up for renewal.

  • Matched on more than price

    The cheapest quote is rarely the best fill. Measured quality, counterparty risk and settlement terms are all part of the match.

  • Settled on the record

    Every quote, match and settlement is tied to a verified counterparty. Nothing on OMDM trades anonymously.

The book

What actually moves a quote

A wholesale base rate goes in. Six families of live signal — 109 in total — push it up or down. A quote comes out, and every step that got it there is visible. No spread hidden in a footnote.

Who trades

A market needs someone
on the other side

OMDM is open to anyone who passes the checks — including the people we compete with. Depth is what makes a market price well.

Operators & MNOs

Sell capacity that would otherwise sit idle overnight, at what it is worth in that hour rather than at an annual average.

MVNOs & resellers

Buy at a market rate instead of whatever a single upstream happens to quote, and switch when a better fill appears.

Enterprises & IoT

Fleet and device connectivity priced continuously, rather than renegotiated once a year and forgotten in between.

And yes

Our competitors are welcome in the book

Bring your own volume in, resell it, or buy from it. A deeper book prices better for everyone standing in it — so we would rather have you quoting here than quoting against us somewhere else. That is the difference between running a market and publishing a price list.
