Install your eSIM.

Use a compatible, carrier-unlocked phone on stable private Wi-Fi.

    Menu names can vary with your device and OS.

    A smooth first connection.

    • Choose the right moment. Activate just before departure or on arrival. Your validity starts as soon as you confirm activation.
    • Use private Wi-Fi. Avoid airport/public Wi-Fi and 4G/5G for installation: these networks may block profile download or activation.
    • Keep data on Openline. Set Openline as your primary Mobile/Cellular Data line and turn automatic data switching OFF.
    • Roam with the right line. Enable Data Roaming for Openline, not your home SIM. Turn off airplane mode and open a browser to check the connection.

    These are instructions, not detected or changed phone settings.
