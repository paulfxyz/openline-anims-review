    A smooth first connection.

    • Choose the right moment. Activate just before departure or on arrival. Your validity starts as soon as you confirm activation.
    • Use private Wi-Fi. Avoid airport/public Wi-Fi and 4G/5G for installation: these networks may block profile download or activation.
    • Make Openline your primary data line. Choose the Openline eSIM for Mobile/Cellular Data.
    • Keep other lines off mobile data. Turn OFF mobile data on your other SIMs/eSIMs and disable automatic data switching. You can keep other lines available for calls and texts.
    • Enable roaming on Openline. Turn Data Roaming ON for the Openline eSIM, not your home SIM.
    • Refresh the connection. Toggle airplane mode ON, then OFF. Allow the phone to reconnect and open a webpage to check.
