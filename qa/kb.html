Two help modals, opened from anywhere.

The knowledge base and the device-compatibility check are the same two modals everywhere on the site — the chat, the installation guide, checkout, an email link. This page shows every way to open them, each one live.

02

For the developers — four ways in

One script on the page; everything else is markup. Each “Try” button runs the snippet beside it.

Markup attributes

Any button or link. Add a search, a topic or an article to land deeper.

<button data-ol-open="kb">
<a data-ol-open="kb" data-ol-q="refund">
<a data-ol-open="kb" data-ol-cat="Troubleshooting">
<a data-ol-open="kb" data-ol-article="what-is-openline">
<button data-ol-open="compat" data-ol-q="iPhone 15">

URL hash

Shareable. Opens on load and on change, and is cleared when the modal closes.

openline.com/#kbTry
openline.com/#kb=japan coverageTry
openline.com/#kb/article/what-is-openlineTry
openline.com/#compat=galaxy s24Try
openline.com/#chatTry

JavaScript

For app flows — a failed install, a checkout step, Gary handing over.

Openline.open('kb', { q: 'esim wont activate' })
Openline.open('compat', { q: 'Pixel 9' })
Openline.open('chat', { topic: 'Refund' })
Openline.close()closes the open modal

Keyboard & embed

⌘K / Ctrl K opens the knowledge base anywhere. ↑ ↓ move through results, Enter opens, Esc closes.

<script type="module"
  src="/qa/support/support.js"></script>

Data loads only on first open, so pages that never open a modal pay nothing.

03

What changed from the live modals

  • Read without losing your place. Results stay on the left and the article opens on the right, with related articles and a “Chat with us” hand-off. On phones it slides in as a second screen.
  • A verdict, not a label. Picking a device gives a clear works / doesn’t-work answer, the regional notes, the *#06# EID check and the next step — find a plan, or ask us.
  • It guesses your phone. On a phone, the compatibility check offers the device it detects as the first thing to check.
  • Real data. Both run on the open-source data: openline-kb (2,737 articles) and openline-check (9,496 devices). The live site says 2,180 articles — that figure is out of date.
