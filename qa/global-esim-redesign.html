What is an eSIM ?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card that's built directly into your smartphone. Instead of inserting a physical SIM card, you simply scan a QR code to activate mobile service. It's the modern way to connect - faster, easier, and more flexible.

Technology Explained

No Physical SIM Card

Everything is digital - no tiny cards to swap, lose, or damage.

Instant Activation

Scan a QR code and you're connected in 30 seconds. No stores, no shipping.

Multiple Plans on One Device

Store multiple eSIM plans and switch between them easily.

Perfect for Travel

Buy before you travel, activate when you land. No hunting for SIM cards abroad.
