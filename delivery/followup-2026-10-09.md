## Help modal: the composer bug is reproducible

The [reported screenshot](https://snap.paulfleury.com/YdlXYxrW) shows clipped placeholder text in the chat message input. It does not establish that an external Typeform embed is involved.

Opening support from the [current revisions site](https://openline-revisions-hub.vercel.app/network), then resizing from 1440 × 1000 to 1440 × 900 and 375 × 812, produced a textarea with a 24px rendered height, 24.65px line height, 20px vertical padding and 45px scroll height. The required single-line content therefore exceeds its rendered height.

The source fix supplied here is in `qa/support/support.css` and `qa/support/chat.js`:

- **CSS floor:** explicit border-box sizing and minimum height of one line plus vertical padding, with the existing 180px maximum.
- **Autosizing:** use the greater of the computed minimum and `scrollHeight`, capped at the maximum.
- **Resize handling:** recalculate when the viewport or composer width changes, including sidebar toggles.
- **Lifecycle:** disconnect the observer and resize listener on close; preserve multiline scrolling and the existing send/attachment/voice behaviour.

Irina must port the fix into her build and return evidence for empty, single-line and multiline input, open/closed sidebar, viewport changes, 200% zoom and the real mobile keyboard. This update fixes the reference source; it does not itself redeploy the revisions hub.
